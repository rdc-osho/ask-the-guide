# Brightwater Home Services: Agent Operations Guide

Brightwater is a fictional company. This guide was written for this project so the assistant has something realistic to search.

## G01 Booking a new installation
New installations for boilers, heat pumps, air conditioning and water heaters are booked in the Scheduler. Confirm the property postcode, the type of product, and whether the customer owns the property. Tenants need written permission from the landlord before an installation can be booked. Offer the earliest slot first, then two alternatives. Installations need a 4 hour window, so never book them into a 2 hour slot. Send the confirmation email from the Scheduler before ending the call.

## G02 Rescheduling or cancelling an appointment
Customers can move an appointment for free if they give at least 48 hours notice. With less notice, a rebooking fee of 35 pounds applies unless the customer is vulnerable or the delay was caused by us. To cancel an installation completely, the customer must confirm by email, and you must log the reason code in the CRM. Always check the appointment date out loud with the customer before saving, because wrong dates are the most common booking error.

## G03 Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 10 working days to appear. Agents can approve refunds up to 250 pounds. Anything above 250 pounds needs team leader approval through the Refund Request form. Never promise a refund date shorter than 5 working days. If the original card has expired, the bank usually redirects the money to the new card automatically.

## G04 Rental agreements
Rental agreements run for a minimum of 5 years. Monthly rental includes parts, labour and an annual service. Customers can buy out the rental early using the buyout table in the CRM, which reduces each year. If the customer sells the house, the rental can be transferred to the new owner with a transfer form signed by both parties.

## G05 Outright purchases and finance
Customers who buy outright pay a 20 percent deposit at booking and the balance on the day of installation. Finance is offered through our credit partner over 3 to 10 years. Agents must never give an opinion on whether finance is affordable. Read the finance script word for word and send the customer the partner's link to apply.

## G06 Warranty
New products come with a 10 year parts warranty and a 2 year labour warranty, as long as the annual service is completed each year. If a service is missed, the parts warranty still applies but labour is charged. Warranty claims are logged as a Warranty Job in the Scheduler with the serial number from the unit.

## G07 No heat or no hot water emergencies
If a customer has no heat or no hot water, treat it as urgent. Ask whether anyone in the property is elderly, very young, unwell or disabled. If yes, book a priority engineer visit within 24 hours. If the customer smells gas, stop the call flow and tell them to call the National Gas Emergency number straight away, then log the call as a safety event.

## G08 Complaints
Log every complaint in the CRM within the same day, even if it is resolved on the call. Use the complaint category list and add a short summary in the customer's own words. If the complaint is not resolved within 3 working days, it moves to the Customer Relations team. Never argue about whether something counts as a complaint. If the customer says they are unhappy, log it.

## G09 Vulnerable customers
A customer may be vulnerable because of age, health, disability, money worries or a recent life event such as a bereavement. Add a Vulnerability flag in the CRM with the customer's permission and note what support they need, for example a call before the engineer arrives. Vulnerable customers are exempt from the rebooking fee.

## G10 Data protection and identity checks
Before discussing an account, confirm the customer's full name, the first line of their address and their date of birth. Never read card numbers back to customers and never write them in notes. If someone calls for a relative, they need to be added as an authorised contact first. Data requests, such as asking for a copy of their data, go to the Privacy team mailbox.

## G11 Engineer running late
If the engineer is more than 30 minutes late, check live tracking in the Scheduler and call the customer with an updated time. If the engineer will miss the window completely, offer the next available slot and a 20 pound goodwill credit. Log the delay with the reason so the operations team can see patterns.

## G12 Annual service
The annual service is included for rental customers and costs 89 pounds for customers who bought outright. Services are booked in the Scheduler as a 2 hour slot. Remind customers that missing a service affects the labour warranty.

## G13 Escalating to a team leader
Escalate to a team leader when a refund is over 250 pounds, a customer asks for a manager twice, there is a safety concern, or you have been on the same issue for more than 20 minutes without progress. Use the Escalate button in the CRM so the case history goes with it, and tell the customer the expected callback time, which is within 4 working hours.

## G14 Callback list for lost sales
When a sale is lost because of a booking error, such as a wrong date, add the customer to the Callback list in the CRM. The callback team contacts the list daily and rebooks. Add a note explaining what went wrong so the same error can be fixed.

## G15 Price matching
We match a written quote from an accredited installer for the same product and the same scope of work. The quote must be less than 30 days old. Agents can match up to 10 percent below our list price. Anything beyond 10 percent goes to a team leader.

## G16 Smart thermostats
Smart thermostats can be added to any installation for 149 pounds, or 3 pounds a month on a rental. Customers set them up with the Brightwater app. If the app will not connect, ask them to restart the router and the thermostat before booking a technician.
